What the buttons do

  • Order here — one tap confirms the suggested shack and takes you to order (happy path for first-time visitors).
  • Change — opens the same location picker the site already uses (nearest shacks first, with search and all locations). Pick any shack and it’s saved.
  • × — dismisses the suggestion without choosing. If you opened the picker with Change instead, closing the picker (the × or tapping outside) counts as that same one-time dismissal — the card won’t come back. It only appears on a first visit, and never again once a shack is confirmed, picked, or dismissed.

Where it appears — a bottom sheet on the first phone visit only. After that the black bar shows your shack name (a link to that location page) plus the NEW! line. To change shack on mobile without the sheet: Menu → Locations → View Details (same as today’s live site).

Good to know

  • Once, not always. The suggestion is for first-time visitors — after a shack is confirmed, chosen, or dismissed, it stays away. Returning customers just see the normal site with their shack at the top.
  • Nothing new to build for the picker. The Change button opens the picker the desktop “Change Your Shack” button already uses — it just needs something on the phone to open it.
  • Honest distances. The mileage in the picker comes from the phone’s location so the list can sort nearest-first. If the location can’t be detected, the picker quietly drops the “Nearby” label and the miles — it just shows search and all locations instead, no wrong guesses. The Locations page stays one tap away in the menu.
