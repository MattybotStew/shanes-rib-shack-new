What the buttons do

  • View Location — one tap confirms the suggested shack and takes you straight to that shack’s page (hours, directions, pickup & delivery). This is the happy path for a first-time visitor.
  • Change — opens the same location picker the site already uses (nearest shacks first, with search and all locations). Pick any shack and it’s saved.
  • × — dismisses the suggestion without choosing. It won’t nag: it only appears on a first visit, and never again once a shack is confirmed or picked.

Where it appears — a small card at the bottom on the first phone visit. After that, the black bar shows your shack; tap the underlined name any time to open the nearby picker (2 taps, no typing).

Good to know

  • Once, not always. The suggestion is for first-time visitors — after a shack is confirmed, chosen, or dismissed, it stays away. Returning customers just see the normal site with their shack at the top.
  • Nothing new to build for the picker. The Change button opens the picker the desktop “Change Your Shack” button already uses — it just needs something on the phone to open it.
  • Honest suggestion. If we can’t detect a location, the card quietly doesn’t show — no wrong guesses, and the Locations page stays one tap away in the menu.
